Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026 | PDF PrintFlow Software

At PDF PrintFlow Pro, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We stand firmly behind the performance, speed, and reliability of our software. This Refund Policy explains your rights regarding refunds and subscription cancellations.

1. 14-Day Free Evaluation Period

We believe in "try before you buy." We encourage all users to download our 14-day fully-featured free trial before purchasing a paid subscription. The trial allows you to connect your production printers, test batch workflows, verify physical odometers, and evaluate compatibility without paying anything upfront.

2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you purchase a subscription (Monthly or Annual) and encounter a technical issue or incompatibility that our technical support team cannot resolve within 14 calendar days of your initial purchase, you are eligible for a 100% full refund.

To request a refund under our 14-day guarantee:

  • Contact our support team at support@pdfprintflowpro.com or via WhatsApp at +20 102 811 1558.
  • Provide your order receipt number, Machine ID, and a brief description of the technical issue encountered.
  • Once approved, refunds are processed immediately back to your original payment method via Lemon Squeezy (funds typically appear within 3 to 7 business days depending on your bank).

3. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your recurring subscription at any time without penalty or cancellation fees:

  • You can cancel directly through your Lemon Squeezy customer billing portal or by contacting support.
  • Upon cancellation, your subscription will remain active and fully functional until the end of your prepaid billing period.
  • After the prepaid billing period concludes, the license key will expire and no further recurring charges will be made.

4. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds are not granted in the following cases:

  • Refund requests submitted after 14 calendar days from the date of initial purchase.
  • Change of mind after extensive commercial production use without any reported technical defect.
  • Subsequent renewal billing cycles where cancellation was not requested prior to the renewal date (although we can immediately prevent future renewals).

5. Contact Our Billing Team

If you need assistance with an invoice, billing question, or refund request, please reach out to us:

  • Email: billing@pdfprintflowpro.com
  • WhatsApp: +20 102 811 1558
  • Help Center: https://pdfprintflowpro.com
