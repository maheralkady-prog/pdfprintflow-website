FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 14-day free trial work? ▼
When you download and run PDF PrintFlow Pro, you automatically get 14 days of unrestricted access to all features on your PC. No credit card is required upfront.
Can I use it on multiple PCs? ▼
Each license key is securely bound to the unique hardware ID (Machine ID) of one computer. If you upgrade your PC, our support team can migrate your license immediately.
What printers and brands are supported? ▼
PDF PrintFlow Pro works natively with all Windows-certified printers, including Ricoh, Xerox, Canon, HP, Konica Minolta, Epson, Brother, Sharp, Kyocera, and digital plotters.
Does the software require an internet connection, and can it print to network printers? ▼
No internet connection is required for printing! PDF PrintFlow Pro operates completely locally on your computer with zero cloud dependency. It connects seamlessly to both local network printers (LAN / Wi-Fi / IP) and direct USB printers. Even if your PC is connected to the internet, your documents and customer data never leave your local environment.
