Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026 | PDF PrintFlow Software

At PDF PrintFlow Pro, we take your privacy and the confidentiality of your print data seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our desktop software and website (PDF PrintFlow Pro).

1. Local Architecture, Network Printer Support & Zero Document Collection

PDF PrintFlow Pro is engineered as a local execution desktop application for commercial print shops and digital copy centers:

  • Zero Cloud Uploads: We NEVER upload, view, transmit, or store your PDF documents, customer files, print jobs, or receipts on any remote cloud server.
  • Network & USB Printer Support: All file rendering, rasterization, page counting, and printer spooling occur strictly on your local PC and local area network (LAN / Wi-Fi / IP). You can print to network-shared production presses without needing internet access.
  • Data Confidentiality: Even when your PC is connected to the internet, your print queues, customer lists, and financial records remain strictly stored on your local workstation.

2. Information We Collect for Licensing

To deliver software licenses and enforce hardware authentication, we collect minimal technical details:

  • Machine Identifier (Machine ID):** An anonymized, one-way cryptographic hash derived from non-sensitive hardware components (e.g. CPU/Motherboard IDs) used solely to lock your subscription key to your designated workstation.
  • Billing Details:** Order transactions are handled by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. We receive confirmation of purchase, billing name, and contact email. We never store or have access to your full credit card numbers or banking passwords.

3. Use of Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Issue and cryptographically sign your active license keys.
  • Send critical version update notifications and security announcements.
  • Provide direct technical customer support via WhatsApp or email upon your request.

4. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances. Payment processing data is shared solely with certified financial processors (Lemon Squeezy, Stripe) to execute authorized payment transactions.

5. Security of Your Information

We employ industry-standard HMAC-SHA256 digital signatures and Windows DPAPI local encryption to safeguard your license credentials and configurations on your workstation.
